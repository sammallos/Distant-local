## `last-light.html` — Last Light

The fall campaign shoot at **San Onofre (Sano)**: a session at Old Man's, a
fire, a cast-iron cook, sunset. Contains no retainer pricing.

| Option | Scope | Fee | All-in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Sundown | Half day, stills only | $1,000 | ~$1,430 |
| B — Firelight | Half day, stills + 4 verticals | $2,200 | ~$3,195 |
| **C — Bonfire** | Full day, 80 stills, hero film, 8 verticals | **$3,400** | ~$4,445 |
| D — Season | Two days, anthem + 12 verticals | $5,900 | ~$8,540 |

All-in includes recommended talent, assistant, park entry, and consumables.

**The blocker to solve first:** San Onofre Surf Beach day-use closes at sunset
and beach fires aren't permitted on the sand — exactly the window blocks 5–8
need. The document proposes three fixes in order (Bluffs campsite with a fire
ring; split the location to Doheny/Calafia for the fire; or camp stove and
lanterns) and flags all of them as needing confirmation with State Parks
before a date is picked.

Also covers: mood board, eight-beat treatment, light-window graphic timed to a
mid-October Sano sunset (~6:15p), run of day, safety, weather fallbacks, terms.
