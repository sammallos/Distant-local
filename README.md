# Distant Local — proposals

Two separate client-facing documents. They stand alone and are sent independently.
Both price off one rate card.

## Rate card

| Engagement | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Half day, photo | $1,000 |
| **Half day, photo + video** | **$2,200** |
| Full day, photo | $1,800 |
| Full day, photo + video | $3,200 |
| Video-led day | $3,800–4,500 |
| Additional day, same trip | −15% |
| Trip anthem film | $1,500 |

Video roughly doubles a half day because the cost is post, not shoot time —
grade, sound design, vertical reframes, audio pass. That's also why the full
day is only $1,000 above the half day: the fixed post cost is already paid.

## `retainer.html` — Pins on the Map

The monthly programme. The unit is a **pin**: one location, one half-day
production block, delivered as 1 film (45–60s), 4 verticals, 20 stills,
2 map cards, 1 audio bed — exactly what $2,200 buys off retainer.

| Tier | Pins | Rate card | Retainer | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Waypoint | 1 | $2,200 | $2,200 | — |
| **Crossing** | 2 | $4,400 | **$3,000** | 32% |
| Meridian 3 | 3 + anthem | $8,100 | $5,000 + travel | 38% |
| Meridian 4 | 4 + anthem | $10,300 | $6,500 + travel | 37% |

The Crossing discount is paid for by **pairing** — two locations within an hour
of each other get shot on one full day, published two weeks apart. Shortlists
are built in geographic pairs for this reason.

Meridian costs slightly *more* per pin than Crossing, not less. International is
harder, not more efficient; what it buys is somewhere you can't drive to plus an
anthem film that only exists because all four were shot on one trip. Two modes:

- **Short-haul** — $6,500 all-in, travel included to a $1,400 cap.
- **Long-haul** — fee plus travel at cost (Australia trip: $4,650–6,500 travel).
  Start at three locations, not four. A fixed all-in alternative is $11,000.

## `last-light.html` — Last Light

The one-off fall campaign shoot: bonfire, surf, cast-iron cook, sunset.

| Option | Scope | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Sundown | Half day, stills only | $1,000 |
| B — Firelight | Half day, stills + 4 verticals | $2,200 |
| **C — Bonfire** | Full day, hero film + 6 verticals, assistant | **$3,400** |

Plus mood board, eight-beat treatment, light-window graphic, run of day,
crew and per diems, logistics and terms. Contains no retainer pricing — that
lives entirely in `retainer.html`.
